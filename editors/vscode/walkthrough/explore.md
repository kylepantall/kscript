## Find out what you can write

- On an empty line, press `Ctrl+Space` to see what can go there: `echo`, `if`, `for`, `fn` come first.
- Type `->` after a value to see what it can do:

```kscript
name = "Joan"
echo $name->upper()
```

- Hover over a word such as `upper` to see what it does, with an example. **Open docs** at the end of the
  hover opens the full docs on it.
